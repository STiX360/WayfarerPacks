# Leather Texture

`textures/wayfarer_packs/leather.png` is an original bitmap generated with the
built-in image generation tool, then copied unchanged into this mod and its
browser preview. No other mod's textures or models are redistributed.

Prompt: Square seamless tileable diffuse/albedo texture for a weathered leather
backpack in a Morrowind-style early-2000s fantasy RPG. Flat orthographic scan of
aged medium grey-neutral leather, edge-to-edge. Fine irregular leather grain,
subtle creases, tiny pores, rubbed and scuffed areas, restrained hand-painted
organic surface detail. Uniform diffuse lighting; no directional shadows,
reflections, objects, bag, seams, borders, text, or logos. Neutral light grey base
for mesh tinting brown, moss green, or charcoal. Seamless matching edges.
