# Wayfarer Packs

Wearable satchels and backpacks for OpenMW, with Feather bonuses and optional
leather crafting and quality-scaled Artisan upgrades.

Buy three normal packs from Arrille: Feather 25, 50, and 75. Wear one at a time
without occupying clothing or armor slots. Packs have no separate storage inventory.
Duplicates remain separate items. Missing normal stock replenishes when Arrille
becomes active again; Artisan packs are crafting-only.

Requires OpenMW 0.51 or newer and Morrowind. Enable `WayfarerPacks.esp`, then
`WayfarerPacks.omwscripts`. Crafting Framework and Inventory Extender are optional.
When using crafting, load `CraftingFramework.omwscripts` before this mod's scripts.

Artisan's Touch consumes a diamond, doubles crafting time, and freezes Feather at
rounded base Feather times crafting quality. At governing skill 100, effects are
38/71/101. Skill Framework's crafting-skill replacement is supported through CF.

Original procedural models and generated leather texture. Straps are rigid Spine1
attachments, not animated cloth; clipping varies with poses, bodies, and armor.
Generated-pack disk save/reload and broader visual compatibility remain pending.
Unequip before uninstalling. See the included README and texture provenance.
